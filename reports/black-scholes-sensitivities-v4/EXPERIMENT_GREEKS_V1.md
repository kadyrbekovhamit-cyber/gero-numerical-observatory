# Analytic sensitivities v1 — local confirmation protocol

Written 26 September 2026 before new confirmation outputs.
Authors: Khamit Kadyrbekov and Daniyal Kadirbekov. Substantial AI assistance.

## Scope and claims

The frozen v2/v3 price evaluators remain unchanged. A separate dependency-free
binary64 API computes model call Delta, put Delta, Gamma, Vega per unit sigma,
their signed/absolute logs, and inverse Vega. It does not differentiate the
restricted ATM price leaf, branch instructions, or rounded price differences.
This is established BSM mathematics; novelty and superiority to current
specialist libraries are not claimed. No price engine speed claim transfers.

Finite T>0 and sigma>0 are required. Rounded total volatility must remain
positive and finite; d1-squared and q*T must be finite. Boundaries and ranges
outside this declared arithmetic contract raise explicit exceptions.
An ordinary Greek may overflow or round to zero; it has a range tag and
a logarithm. An infinite inverse Vega is not a recovered volatility.

## Development, then freeze

All disclosed v1/v2/v3 price inputs (316+288+480) and the 60 exploratory
finite-difference stencils are development. The latter compare exact model
derivatives, high-precision unequal-grid stencils, rounded-reference-price
stencils, and frozen v2/v3 prices. They do not isolate a defect merely because
Gamma becomes negative at a tiny step. The ATM branch derivative is an analytic
counterexample to an unsafe future AD port, not an executed native AD feature.

After development passes, freeze candidate, protocol, runner, oracle, corpus
generator, tests, dependencies, mpmath sources, worker and runtime hashes.
384 new exact-binary64 input tuples are generated by seed 2026092604:

- 96 regular positive-volatility cases;
- 96 neighborhoods of the price engine's switches;
- 96 tail/monetary/discount range cases;
- 48 exact-ATM cases, including very small representable volatility;
- 48 neighborhoods of the new normal-tail representation switch.

Deduplicate against v1/v2/v3 and within the new corpus. Family membership is
a construction label, not proof of which rounded numerical branch is selected.
The pipeline is frozen locally, not externally preregistered. After outputs,
retain every row and any failure. Changes require a new version/new confirmation.

## Reference and gates

Primary reference: exact conversion of the six binary64 inputs to mpmath,
then analytic BSM sensitivities, separately at 100 and 180 digits. Require
relative convergence <1e-60 and scaled-log convergence <1e-60. This is a
precision-stability check, not a certified interval oracle. Three unit fixtures
also differentiate the unspecialized high-precision BSM price expression.

For each of five ordinary outputs, when the rounded reference is finite:

`abs(error) <= max(2e-11 * abs(exact_reference), 1 ULP(rounded_reference))`.

Require the correct normal/subnormal/rounded-zero/overflow class, no zero
when the correctly rounded value is nonzero, and the correct signed infinity
if the mathematical output rounds beyond binary64. Overflow cases are counted
separately, never included in normal relative-error summaries.

For each of five logarithms:

`abs(error) <= max(2e-11, 32 ULP(rounded_reference_log))`.

These are mixed practical gates, not a universal relative, one-ULP, or
32-ULP accuracy assertion. Report all field counts, failures, oracle checks,
range strata and normal maxima. No timing contest is included.

## IV and publication limits

The separate rounding example uses S=200,K=100,T=1,r=q=0. Correctly rounded
call=100 can represent multiple positive sigmas. The upper transition is
estimated by high-precision bisection of put(sigma)=0.5*ULP(100).
The bisection is illustrative, not a certified interval-valued IV solver.
No market quotes, bid/ask observations, trading return, hedging backtest,
calibration, human peer review, or MSU endorsement are claimed.

All local computation is sequential through the shared one_worker, one
configured numerical thread, no GPU or new heavy dependency installation.

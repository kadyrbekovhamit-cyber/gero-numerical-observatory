Causal attention should not read the future. A three-value test shows where that contract fails.

In nntrainer’s native AttentionLayer, with causal_mask=true, zero logits, two queries and V=[2, 10, 50], the original output is [20.66666794, 20.66666794]. Under the layer’s upper-left causal convention, the target is [2, 6]. Changing the final value also changes earlier outputs.

One implementation report, three related mechanisms:
• A square mask cannot be added to rectangular scores; the error is ignored.
• A finite −1e10 penalty can be overcome by large finite logits.
• Later incremental blocks skip causal masking.

The proposed repair directly sets forbidden scores to −infinity, uses actual score dimensions and accounts for the absolute query offset.
